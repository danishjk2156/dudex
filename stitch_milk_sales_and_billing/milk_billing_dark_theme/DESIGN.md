# Milk Sales & Billing App Design System

## Brand Identity
A warm, professional dairy business billing app. The design should feel trustworthy, fast, and modern while being easy to use for daily operations.

## Color Philosophy
- Primary amber/gold tones represent warmth and the dairy industry
- Dark backgrounds for comfortable extended use
- High contrast for readability in bright outdoor/shop environments

## Key UI Patterns
- Cards with subtle glassmorphism effect
- Bottom navigation bar (5 tabs)
- Tap-friendly product grid tiles (min 48px touch targets)
- Monospace JetBrains Mono for receipt/billing numbers
- Floating action buttons for primary actions
- Smooth transitions between states
- Toast notifications for confirmations

## Mobile-First
- Designed primarily for mobile phones (360-414px width)
- Touch-optimized controls
- Minimal text entry, maximum tap-to-select
